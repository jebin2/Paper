About Paper

  • What is it? A private scratchpad. Each note lives at its own random, unguessable link and is locked with your passphrase.
  • Encryption: Notes are encrypted in your browser. Your passphrase and the readable text never leave your device — the server only stores encrypted data.
  • Zero Access: Even the developer cannot read your notes.
  • Auto-Delete: Notes are deleted after 2 days without being opened or edited.
  • No recovery: Lose the link or the passphrase and the note is gone. Use a long passphrase — 16+ characters.
  • Open Source: Every line is public on GitHub.
